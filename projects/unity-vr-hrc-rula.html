VR Training Scenarios for Human-Robot Collaboration Hazards

three interactive vr scenarios built in unity for a civil engineering course, teaching students to recognize human-robot collaboration hazards on a job site before they ever run into them in real life: skipping the robot-arm e-stop, an unsafe generator shutdown, and a structural inspection hazard. built for the meta quest 3, made as a sample for job applications.

Scenarios:

What I Did

  • built the core gameplay systems in C#: character/object animation, state-based scenario logic, and XR grab/interaction controls
  • added a hazard-logging system so instructors can review what each student did during a scenario
  • tested and debugged on a Meta Quest 3, iterating with the research team until each scenario ran reliably for classroom use

Tech Stack

Unity, C#, XR Interaction Toolkit, Meta Quest 3
